A mix of wide, close, action, and behind-the-scenes shots of you performing your sport. Add a short note with the sport, location, and context.

02 / ACTION PHOTO

One high-resolution sport photo

Choose a sharp image that shows you in your sporting environment. Only send photos you own or have permission to license.

One clear portrait

Face visible, good light, and minimal background clutter. A recent phone portrait is fine if the original is high resolution.

04 / TESTIMONIAL

2–4 honest sentences

Describe what you actually tested, what was useful, and who you think it fits. It does not need to be positive if that was not your experience.

For paid creator assignments

Clear standards before anyone presses record.

Your individual written brief controls the deliverables, fee, deadline, and licensed uses. Free testing access alone does not promise payment.

01

Objective acceptance

We check the agreed format, resolution, audio, readable app result, real app use, disclosure, message, clean files, and deadline—not whether the video gets a certain number of views.

02

One reasonable correction

If a delivery materially misses the written brief, TrackSpeed will identify the issue and give one reasonable opportunity to reshoot or correct it within an agreed window.

03

50% kill fee

If the corrected delivery still materially fails the agreed requirements and cannot reasonably be used, 50% of the agreed production fee is paid instead of the full fee. Compliant work earns the full fee.

Payment is not reduced for low views, an honest opinion, or subjective stylistic preference. If TrackSpeed cancels or changes direction after compliant delivery, the agreed full production fee remains due.

Keep the message credible

Talk about what you tested, not what you cannot prove.

Free access is not payment for a positive review. If the app does not feel reliable in your session, send feedback instead of forcing a post.

Say this
  • What sprint or drill you tested.
  • What happened in your actual session.
  • How you would use TrackSpeed in your own training.
  • That TrackSpeed provided free Pro access.
Avoid this
  • FAT-certified, Olympic-grade, or official-result claims.
  • “Perfectly accurate in every setup.”
  • A result you did not actually record.
  • Promises about discounts, payouts, or audience codes that were not agreed.
Simple disclosure example “TrackSpeed gave me free Pro access to test the app.” Follow the disclosure rules for your platform and arrangement.

Before you send or post

Make the result readable. Keep the rights clear.

Upload the agreed files, testimonial, action photo, and headshot to your private folder. TrackSpeed will confirm separately—in writing—where each asset may appear, for how long, and whether paid advertising is included.

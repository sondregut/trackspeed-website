# TrackSpeed website direction — 2026-09-06

The two generated compositions guide this implementation. Both use pale daylight surfaces, dark neutral typography, unboxed product cutouts, compact pill controls, and generous spacing. The top composition gives the timing phone the strongest visual weight, with the actual Home screen behind it. The continuation uses one wide landscape phone, a separate Watch story, then history and sharing screens.

Implementation decisions:
- Keep the hero to two short lines, one short paragraph, and two actions. Remove the earlier boxed stage, ornamental track lines, fact strip, and eyebrow text.
- Use a 1240 px content measure with roughly 100 px between sections. Device imagery is larger than the accompanying text and retains its full outline.
- Use the new original athlete imagery throughout. The track, football, and indoor images share the real TrackSpeed timing card and red crossing line; the gallery switches instantly with no automatic rotation.
- Keep the public setup selector to Solo Laps and Connected Gates. The generated composition mixes a landscape drill into this section; the implementation deliberately avoids that availability mismatch.
- Give landscape and Watch their own development sections. Retain clear development status and the paired-iPhone requirement; no inferred release or accuracy claims.
- Preserve actual native screenshots for Home, History, and sharing. The generated comps are visual references, not flattened website content.
- Retain all five public start types, feature-guide access, founder information, FAQs, and store destinations. Use shorter Home copy; longer explanations stay in expandable details and the feature guide.
- Match the calm visual direction at narrow widths, stacking copy above images and preserving 44 px controls, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and RTL behavior.

References reviewed directly: https://www.bevel.health/ and https://metric.coach/sprint. Their spacing, product emphasis, and restraint informed the direction. TrackSpeed imagery, text, UI, and branding are original.

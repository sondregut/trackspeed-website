# Design references — 6 September 2026

Reviewed the live pages and desktop layouts at the owner's request:

- [Bevel](https://www.bevel.health/): a compact floating navigation, generous spacing, large app imagery, soft backgrounds, and short explanations organized around user benefits. Feature sections show recognizable screens rather than relying on icons alone.
- [Metric Sprint](https://metric.coach/sprint): a clear sprint-specific introduction, alternating screenshots and explanations, and contextual links to setup and metric guides. Its prominent diagrams/screens help connect the description to the actual training task.

## Applied to TrackSpeed

- A compact floating navigation with a clear download action and accessible mobile menu.
- A larger composition of real TrackSpeed screens on a pale blue surface. The TrackSpeed wordmark, palette, athlete photography, and app assets remain its own.
- Dedicated visual stories for session history and sharing, with large real screens, short captions, and links to the relevant feature guide entries.
- Interactive course diagrams and step-by-step instructions for selecting a setup.
- A complete searchable feature inventory supports the shorter homepage story.
- Quiet color/focus feedback; no autoplay, scroll hijacking, or repeated motion in these new sections.

Competitor product claims are not evidence of TrackSpeed functionality. TrackSpeed copy continues to distinguish average speed from peak velocity and public functionality from Watch/landscape development previews. Reference images, review counts, awards, testimonials, and product claims were not copied into TrackSpeed.
